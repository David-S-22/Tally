Welcome to Tally

Tally is your personal finance dashboard. It brings together a set of small, independent services behind a single shell so you can manage your money from one place.

What you can do

Tab What it's for
Transactions Record and review the transactions you've entered.
Anomalies See transactions an agent has flagged as suspicious, and confirm whether they're genuine.
Savings Track savings goals and get AI-assisted suggestions to reach them.
Bills Keep on top of your upcoming and recurring bills.
Budgets View your monthly budgets and the current budget records available in Tally.

Pick a tab above to get started.
